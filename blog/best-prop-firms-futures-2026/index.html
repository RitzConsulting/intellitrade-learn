Best Prop Firms for Futures Traders in 2026

February 27, 2026 · By IntelliTrade · Prop Firms

The proprietary trading firm landscape has matured significantly over the past few years. What started as a niche alternative to self-funded trading has become one of the most popular paths for futures traders who want access to real capital without risking their own savings. In 2026, there are more options than ever, but the quality gap between firms has also widened.

The core proposition remains the same: you pay a monthly evaluation fee, prove you can trade profitably within defined risk parameters, and the firm funds your account with real capital. You keep a percentage of the profits, typically 80-90%. The key differences between firms come down to evaluation rules, payout speed, profit splits, and how many hoops you have to jump through once funded.

Top 5 Futures Prop Firms

1. Take Profit Trader

Best for: Straightforward rules and fast payouts. Take Profit Trader has built a reputation for transparency. Their evaluation is a single-phase process with clear drawdown limits and no minimum trading days in many of their plans. Once funded, payouts are processed quickly and reliably. The profit split is competitive at 80/20 in the trader's favor, with pathways to increase that to 90/10. The main downside is that their account sizes start smaller than some competitors.

2. Apex Trader Funding

Best for: Aggressive promotions and large account sizes. Apex is one of the most well-known names in the space, largely due to their frequent discount promotions that can bring evaluation costs down significantly. They offer account sizes up to $300K and have a one-step evaluation process. The tradeoff is that their funded account rules can be more restrictive, and some traders report slower payout processing during peak periods.

3. Tradeify

Best for: Simplicity and trader-friendly rules. Tradeify has carved out a niche by keeping things simple. No daily loss limits on many plans, straightforward trailing drawdown mechanics, and a clean dashboard. They are newer to the scene but have been gaining traction quickly. Their profit split starts at 80/20 and their evaluation fees are competitive. Worth watching as they continue to scale.

4. My Funded Futures

Best for: Experienced traders who want flexibility. My Funded Futures offers multiple evaluation paths and account sizes, with some of the more generous drawdown allowances in the industry. Their funded rules give traders room to breathe, which matters when trading volatile instruments like ES and NQ. Payouts are reliable, and their support team is responsive. The evaluation fees are slightly higher than average, but the funded experience makes up for it.

5. Elite Trader Funding

Best for: Budget-conscious traders and beginners. Elite Trader Funding offers some of the lowest evaluation fees in the market, making it a good entry point for traders who want to test the prop firm model without a large upfront investment. Their rules are reasonable, though the profit split and scaling options are less generous than the top-tier firms. A solid choice for getting your feet wet.

Tips for Getting Started

Before diving into a prop firm evaluation, make sure you have a tested strategy with clear rules. Paper trade it first. Most traders fail evaluations not because their strategy is bad, but because they overtrade or ignore risk limits under pressure. Start with the smallest account size to learn the rules, and scale up once you have a funded track record.

For a deeper comparison of rules, fees, and strategies for passing evaluations, check out our complete Prop Firms education page.

Bottom line: The best prop firm for you depends on your trading style, account size needs, and how much you value payout speed versus low evaluation costs. All five firms listed here are legitimate operations that have paid out real profits to traders.
